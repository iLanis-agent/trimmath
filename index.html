Trim math

Baseboard bought by eye means a second trip for one short board. Trim math turns the room's size and doorways into the run of wall to cover, turns the run into a board count with a labeled waste allowance, and turns the corners into mitre cuts and the extra length they eat. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Run plan - length, width and doorways become the meters of wall left to trim.
  • Board plan - the run, the board length and a labeled waste percentage become a board count and the length left over.
  • Corner plan - inside and outside corners become mitre cuts and a labeled extra length for each corner.

Labeled norms

Labeled norms: 10% waste on boards, 5 cm of extra length a corner, 0.9 m for a doorway - all illustrative defaults you can change. Walls are never perfectly square, and real rooms have bay windows, cupboards and uneven floors. This is arithmetic, not a measured quote: measure the real walls and buy a spare board.
